About Driss

I started editing in 2018 on a Galaxy S5, cutting on a phone because that was what I had — from KineMaster and Alight Motion to After Effects on PC. Eight-plus years later I have delivered 2,300+ videos for creators, channels and real estate brands.

The toolkit stays deliberately small: After Effects and CapCut only. No bloated pipeline, just pacing, hooks and cuts your audience can feel — from Fes, Morocco, for clients in the US, UK, EU and Canada.
